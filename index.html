One case engine

Three ways to run a case

Author a case once. Use it in a live event, a timed challenge, or a self-paced path.

Live event

Everyone, at the same time

An expert presents on an embedded stream while participants work the same case under one synchronised timer. A moderator sets the pace and publishes the results.

Challenge

A weekly habit

A case opens for a fixed window. Doctors submit when it suits them, then see the answer, the reasoning and where they rank.

Self-paced

Practice on demand

Past cases, recordings and case series joined into learning paths for a specialty or condition.

Live events

How a live case runs

1

Join

Participants sign in with a mobile number or email and a one-time code. No app install is needed on web.

2

Review

Patient profile, history and high-resolution images sit beside the presenter's stream.

3

Decide

Doctors pick a diagnosis and a treatment approach from standard lists before the timer ends. Answers save automatically.

4

Compare

The moderator publishes results. Each doctor sees their own answer against the room, and the expert leads the discussion.

Built for any specialty

The right viewer for the right evidence

Specialty packs configure the patient fields, viewer tools and answer lists for each field of medicine, so nothing is hard-wired to one domain.

DermatologyRadiologyCardiologyPathologyOphthalmologyGeneral medicine
  • Clinical photographs. Zoom, pan, annotate and compare dated images side by side.
  • Imaging. X-ray, CT and MRI with slice scrolling and contrast presets.
  • ECG, echo and video. Callipers on the trace, frame-by-frame on clips.
  • Labs and reports. Structured values with trends, scanned reports and prescriptions on one timeline.
  • Cases from a PDF. Upload a case document; the platform fills the fields for your team to approve.

Insight and integration

From attendance counts to evidence

  • Live results. Audience answers as charts the moment results are published.
  • Preference reports. How participants would treat a case, segmented by specialty, experience and region.
  • Knowledge gaps. The conditions and questions a cohort most often gets wrong.
  • CRM connectors. Sync participants and engagement to your CRM after each event, at the data-sharing level you set and with participant consent.

Compliance by design

Built for regulated healthcare

Patient privacy, participant consent and editorial independence are controls in the product, not policies beside it.

De-identified cases only

Automated and manual checks run on every image and document before a case is published.

Consent-led data sharing

Reporting is aggregate by default. Anything more needs the participant's consent.

Independent content

Sponsors do not set the diagnosis or the reference answer. Treatments are named generically.

Your rules, per tenant

Data residency, retention and automatic purging are configured for each organisation and market.
